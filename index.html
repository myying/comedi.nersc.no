Horizon Europe · Grant agreement 101296319

COpernicus Marine services with Enhanced Data assimilation and improved Interoperability

COMEDI will modernise and harmonise data assimilation (DA) practices across all Monitoring and Forecasting Centres (MFCs) of the Copernicus Marine Service, enabling them to leverage new technologies and use new types of ocean observations to achieve better accuracy and reliability.
