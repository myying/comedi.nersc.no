COpernicus Marine services with Enhanced Data assimilation and improved Interoperability

COMEDI will modernise and harmonise data assimilation practices across all Monitoring and Forecasting Centres of the Copernicus Marine Service, enabling them to leverage new technologies and use new types of ocean observations to achieve better accuracy and reliability.

Funding
Horizon Europe
Grant agreement no. 101296319
Duration
48 months, from Sep 2026
Coordinator
NERSC, Bergen, Norway
Consortium
11 partners, 7 Monitoring and Forecasting Centres

Four objectives

  • 1Advance data assimilation methods
  • 2Make best use of observations
  • 3Improve interoperability
  • 4Prepare innovations for operational use
