About the project

About COMEDI

COMEDI works at the heart of the Copernicus Marine Service: the step where observations and models are combined into the ocean products that users rely on. Eleven partners, three joint development teams and an external advisory board carry out the work.

Copernicus Marine Service

From observations to users

Every MFC production system has the same building blocks. Data assimilation combines model predictions with observations to find the best estimate of the ocean state, which then starts the forecasts and reanalyses that users rely on.

Observe

Satellites and in situ networks such as Argo floats measure sea level, temperature, salinity, sea ice and ocean colour.

Assimilate and forecast COMEDI

The MFCs combine the observations with ocean, biogeochemistry and sea-ice models through data assimilation.

Deliver products

Copernicus Marine publishes near-real-time forecasts and multi-year reanalyses, with their uncertainties.

Serve users

~30,000 active users, over 100,000 registered

  • Shipping and navigation
  • Fisheries and aquaculture
  • Offshore industries
  • Coastal services
  • Policy makers
  • Environmental NGOs
  • Academia
  • The general public

Partners

COMEDI partners

DA developers from the MFCs work alongside research institutes and universities, so that new methods are taken up in operations.

AI JDT

AI techniques for data assimilation. ULiège, NERSC, CMCC.

Protocol JDT

Diagnostic protocols for observation impact. MOi, ECMWF, UKMET, METNO, OGS.

Software JDT

Interoperable DA software. AWI, NERSC, MOi, CMCC.
